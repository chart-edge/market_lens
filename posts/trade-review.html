TRADING JOURNAL · STARTER ARTICLE

Review a trade without judging it by profit alone

Review your decision-making process separately from the financial outcome.

This is an educational starter article, not a live market recommendation.

Separate process from outcome

A profitable trade can still break your rules, while a losing trade can follow a sound plan. Review what you knew and decided at the time, rather than using the result as the only measure of quality.

Ask the same questions every time

  • Did the setup meet my written criteria?
  • Was the entry based on confirmation or emotion?
  • Was the position size within my risk limits?
  • Did I follow my exit plan?
  • What was within my control, and what was uncertainty?

Look for patterns over a sample

One trade rarely proves a strategy works or fails. Track a meaningful series of trades, keep your rules consistent, and avoid changing the method after every result.

The purpose of a journal is not to justify a trade after the fact. It is to make the next decision more informed.

Journal prompt

Write one thing you executed well, one thing to improve, and one specific action to test in your next review.
