TECHNICAL ANALYSIS · STARTER ARTICLE

Read market structure before searching for an entry

A practical framework for describing price action before deciding which trading scenarios deserve attention.

This is an educational starter article, not a live market analysis or trading signal.

Start with context

Zoom out and identify the sequence of swing highs and swing lows. Is price forming higher highs and higher lows, lower highs and lower lows, or moving sideways? Treat this as a working description of the chart, not a prediction of what must happen next.

Mark only meaningful levels

Look for a small number of levels with a clear reason to matter: recent swing points, range boundaries, or areas where price previously reacted. Too many lines can make random movement look significant.

Write down more than one scenario

  • Describe what supports your current market view.
  • Identify what price action would weaken or invalidate that view.
  • Wait for your predefined confirmation instead of chasing a move.
  • Decide the maximum acceptable risk before considering a trade.
A market bias is a scenario, not a promise. When the evidence is unclear, waiting can be a valid decision.

Journal prompt

Record the instrument, timeframe, market structure, key levels, possible scenarios, invalidation conditions, and one thing you learned after the session.
