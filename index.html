THE MARKET LENS METHOD

Observe. Plan. Review.

A repeatable process matters more than a confident prediction.

01

Observe context

Describe the trend, structure, volatility, and levels without forcing a bias.

02

Build scenarios

Write down what could happen, what would confirm it, and what would invalidate it.

03

Respect risk

Consider position sizing, costs, uncertainty, and the potential for losses.

04

Review evidence

Record what happened and learn from the quality of the decision—not just the outcome.
